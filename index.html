The Anonymous Tip

Somebody slipped this coded note under our door. Can you decode it?

— crumpled note, found Tuesday night —
PHHW PH DW WKH
ROG EDW EDUQ
DW PLGQLJKW
How to decode it — Caesar Cipher, Shift 3
Each letter has been shifted forward by 3 in the alphabet.
To read the real message, shift each letter back by 3.
Example:  D → A  |  H → E  |  P → M
